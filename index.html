01 · The Mark

The three-circle heart.

Raoul’s threefold, freed from the stale diagram: three overlapping circles — each a gradation (pink→orange, yellow→green, blue→violet) — whose edges carve a heart at the center. Body, mind, and nature, with care at the core. Where the circles cross, the colours combine into new hues — primary colour into infinite potential. One mark serves the whole brand; the Center and the Method share it.

Primary logo — Material-based (subtractive) · on warm cream · white heart. The definitive mark.

How it’s built

Two circles above, one below. Their overlap carves the heart; nothing else is drawn. Each circle holds a gradation — pink→orange, yellow→green, blue→violet — and where they cross, the colours multiply into new hues. The heart at the center is always white.

Where it lives

The mark sits on light, warm grounds (cream) — never on dark. It is one identity used in two places: paired with Path to Health for the Center, and with PATH Method for the method. Same mark, different wordmark.

03 · The Three Marks

Primary, secondary, tertiary.

The full-colour mark is the primary and is always preferred. The secondary (shaded greyscale) and tertiary (outline) are for one-colour contexts — book spine, foil, embossing, stamps, very small sizes.

Primary — full colour

Secondary — shaded greyscale · light petals, dark overlaps

Tertiary — outline

Warm precision, real light.

06 · Using the Mark

Do & don’t.

Do

Place the mark on cream or other light, warm grounds. Give it clear space equal to one circle’s radius on every side.

Don’t

Don’t place the mark on dark or black backgrounds — the mark lives on light only.

Do

Keep the heart white, always. It is the fixed center of the identity.

Don’t

Don’t recolour the heart, rotate or flip the mark, or reorder the circles.

Do

Use the single-colour line/greyscale mark when colour isn’t available (spine, foil, tiny sizes).

Don’t

Don’t stretch, add shadows/outlines, or drop the mark onto a busy photo without clear space.
